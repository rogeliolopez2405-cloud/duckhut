Playing on a TV or Xbox

Open this game in your TV browser or Microsoft Edge on your Xbox. Choose TV / Fullscreen above. With a compatible controller: left stick or D-pad aims, A or right trigger shoots, and Menu pauses. Press A again to start or continue. If Xbox Edge offers game controls, enable them.

TV remotes: arrow keys aim and OK / Enter shoots when the game has focus. Browser and remote support varies; a USB mouse or a computer connected to your TV by HDMI is a fallback. This is a browser game, not an installed Xbox app.

Keep Duckhut close.

Choose the shortcut that fits your device.

Xbox

In Edge, open Favorites and choose Add this page to favorites. Next time, open Edge → Favorites → Duckhut. This saves typing the address. Direct installation as an Xbox game is not confirmed.

Phone or computer

If your browser offers Install app or Add to Home Screen, use it for a dedicated shortcut. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

The game saves this edition as the app’s start page when your browser supports installation. Open it online once to prepare offline play. Xbox browser support may differ.
